# Context (public sources)

This is the only document in the repository that names a real organisation. The statements
below are secondary sources: press coverage and a public results release. They motivate the
work; they are not evidence that any organisation uses, endorses or requested Madkhal.
Madkhal does not model any real plant. Its plant is hypothetical, with synthetic parameters.

## Statements that motivate the work

1. **AI for water efficiency.** An article in Aletihad (English edition), dated 17 April
   2025, reports that TAQA's Chief Strategy Officer, speaking at a sustainability forum in Abu
   Dhabi, described the company's use of AI for maintenance forecasting, electricity
   distribution and climate risk modelling, and for improving water efficiency. The article
   does not mention desalination intakes or algal blooms.
   Source: https://en.aletihad.ae/news/business/4567209/taqa-spotlights-how-ai-boosts-efficiency--slash-emissions-ac
   (opened 2026-10-13).

2. **Reverse osmosis share of desalination capacity.** TAQA's full year 2025 results release,
   dated 12 February 2026, states that over 40 percent of the company's desalination capacity
   now uses reverse osmosis technology. The same release reports an agreement to reconfigure
   one cogeneration plant (power and water desalination) to power only. The build brief also
   described the release as saying that ageing thermal desalination units are reaching end of
   life; that wording was **not found** in the text that was opened, so it is not cited here.
   Source: https://apigateway.adx.ae/adx/cdn/1.0/content/download/4698142 (a PDF; the page
   fetcher was refused with HTTP 403, and the file was then downloaded and read on 2026-10-13).

3. **Documented bloom related disruptions.** The events and their evidence statuses are taken
   unchanged from Nadhir's `events/events.yaml` (snapshot in `data/nadhir_snapshot/events.yaml`).
   Most event dates are UNVERIFIED news report dates, which are upper bounds on onset, and all
   intake coordinates are ESTIMATED. Madkhal displays every event with its status.

## Why these statements matter here

A growing share of reverse osmosis capacity raises the importance of pretreatment, which is
where algal blooms do their damage. Interest in AI for water efficiency suggests that decision
support of this kind is a reasonable subject for a student proposal. Neither statement is a
request for, or a validation of, this tool.
